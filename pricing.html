Questions

Before you ask

Why monthly instead of one big payment?+

A site needs hosting, security, and updates for as long as it is live. The subscription rolls all of that into one predictable cost, so you are never left with a site nobody is maintaining.

What changes the price?+

Mainly the number of pages and any extra features like a catalog, booking, or a store. More involved builds take more work to set up and maintain, so they sit higher. You always get a fixed quote first.

Do I have to buy the domain myself?+

Yes. The domain stays in your name so you always own it. We handle connecting it to your site. Domain registration is a small separate cost paid to the registrar.

Can I add or remove things later?+

That is the whole idea. Add a catalog, a booking tool, or extra pages when you need them, and adjust your plan as your business changes.

What if I want to cancel the subscription?+

The site comes down from our hosting. If you want to change hosts and keep the site design, you can contact us to buy out the site for an agreed-upon sum. However, if you sign up for a yearly plan (12 or 24 months), you get to keep the site design and files if you cancel.
