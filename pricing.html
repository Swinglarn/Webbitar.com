Questions

Before you ask

Why a range and not a fixed price?+

A five-page site and a fifteen-page site with a booking tool are very different jobs. The ranges show where most projects land, and the quote pins it down once we know what you actually need.

What affects the final price?+

Number of pages, how much custom design and functionality is involved, whether you have content ready, and any tools or integrations. We walk through all of it before quoting.

Do I have to take the hosting plan?+

No. It's the easiest path because we then handle everything, but you're free to host the finished site wherever you like.

How long does a build take?+

A starter site is usually a couple of weeks; larger and custom builds take longer. We give you a timeline with the quote.
