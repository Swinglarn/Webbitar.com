How to Add to Microsoft Outlook

A. Outlook Desktop (Windows)

  1. Highlight and copy one of the signatures above (or click the copy button).
  2. Open Microsoft Outlook on your computer.
  3. Go to File > Options > Mail > Signatures...
  4. Click New, give your signature a name (e.g. Webbitar Main).
  5. Paste the copied signature into the text box under Edit signature.
  6. Replace [Your Full Name], [Your Job Title / Role], and phone numbers with your actual details.
  7. Under Choose default signature, set it for New messages and Replies/forwards.
  8. Click OK and save.

B. Outlook on the Web / New Outlook for Windows

  1. Click the Settings icon (&gear;) in the top right corner of Outlook.
  2. Go to Accounts > Signatures (or Mail > Compose and reply).
  3. Click + New signature.
  4. Paste the signature, personalize your name, title, and phone number.
  5. Select the signature as default for new messages and replies.
  6. Click Save.
