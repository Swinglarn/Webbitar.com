About Webbitar

A small studio that owns what it builds.

We make websites for businesses, and we run our own network of sites alongside them. That mix keeps us honest: we use the same tools, hosting, and SEO playbook on our own work that we put behind yours.

Most agencies hand you a site and walk away. We do the opposite. We design and build it, we host it, and we keep caring for it so it stays fast, secure, and findable for years, not just on launch day.

Webbitar grew out of building and ranking our own sites. Before we ask a client to trust an approach, we have usually tried it on our own properties first. If it does not bring in traffic for us, we do not sell it to you.
